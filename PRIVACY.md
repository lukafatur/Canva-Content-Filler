# Privacy Policy — Text Populator

**Last updated:** 11 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Text Populator** Canva app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. It is provided by **[YOUR NAME / COMPANY]**.

## Summary

**Text Populator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
The App runs entirely inside the Canva editor in your browser. It has no servers, no database, no analytics, and makes no external network requests.

## What the App accesses

To do its job, the App uses the Canva Apps SDK to:

- **Read text content from your current design page** when you click "Collect", so it can list and export that text.
- **Read and write design content** (text and images) when you create or populate pages, so it can apply your variants.
- **Write to your clipboard** when you click "Copy", so you can paste the text into a spreadsheet.
- **Generate a file download** when you click "Download .csv", so you can save the text locally.

All of this happens locally, between your browser and Canva. The App never sends your design content or any other information to us or to any third party.

## What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect names, email addresses, design content, usage analytics, cookies, or any other personal data.

## Third parties

The App does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party services. The only platform involved is Canva itself, whose handling of your data is governed by [Canva's Privacy Policy](https://www.canva.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

## Data retention

Because the App does not collect or store data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Any text you copy to your clipboard or download as a CSV is under your control on your own device.

## Children's privacy

The App is a productivity tool for use within Canva and is not directed at children.
